# Abstract

## Research Background

Complex outdoor mobile robots often suffer from GNSS degradation caused by vegetation occlusion and environmental interference.

## Research Objective

This thesis focuses on autonomous navigation of mobile robots in GNSS-degraded outdoor environments.

## Technical Approach

A ROS2-based navigation framework integrating 3D LiDAR global localization, FAST-LIO2 continuous tracking, and Nav2 autonomous navigation is investigated.

## Expected Contributions

1. Design of a multi-layer autonomous navigation architecture.
2. Integration of global relocalization and local state estimation.
3. Experimental validation on a tracked mobile robot platform.
